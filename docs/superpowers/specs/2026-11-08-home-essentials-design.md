# Home Essentials website

Confirmed brand: Home Essentials. Amazon.com, US shoppers, USD, US English. Pinterest is the primary discovery source. The latest screenshot supplies the page and feature brief; the earlier brand name in that screenshot is superseded by the user's correction.

## Scope and approach

Use the Sites Vinext/React/TypeScript starter for a responsive editorial affiliate publication. No accounts, checkout, visitor database, newsletter subscription, or analytics. A CMS would add administration outside the present brief; a static one-page site would not provide the requested indexable category and product routes.

Routes: homepage; four categories (kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, organization); all finds/search; product research articles; buying guides; About; Contact; Privacy; Affiliate Disclosure; useful 404 and route error/loading states.

## Visual system

The Pinterest visitor arrives with an idea for a room. Treat the site as an edited home shopping journal, with generously sized room photography, an editorial serif headline, readable sans-serif body, paper ground, dark green ink, and restrained olive actions. Extend those materials to collection headers, product articles, policy pages, and footer. Subject mechanism: a saved room idea becomes a practical shortlist. Live interaction: filter verified finds by room and search, with a clear empty state.

Direction considered: split-stage and page-is-the-object. Adopt the second as an open shopping journal rather than a literal invitation. Redraw type (the dealt condensed signage face does not suit a domestic reading publication) and palette (the previously proposed muted green brand direction). No scroll scenes, fake statistics, testimonials, or ratings. Photographs are room inspiration, not photographs of the researched products, and are captioned accordingly.

## Content and integrity

Every product ASIN must be observed on Amazon.com and every product fact attributed to a primary source on its article. Editorial assessment is distinguished from testing. No copied customer reviews. No numeric prices, stock, discounts, star ratings, or aggregateRating schema without an authorized current data source. Price label: Check current price on Amazon. Regular product links work without a tracking ID. Only a validated tracking ID with explicit affiliate activation adds a tag. Disclosure copy reflects activation status.

Contact email and Pinterest profile are pending owner input. Do not fabricate an address, social profile, successful form submission, or customer support channel. Contact page has a truthful unavailable state until configured.

## Interactions and verification

Semantic navigation with mobile menu, Escape handling, focus support, category filters and search, verified outbound product links, Pinterest sharing of article URLs and room imagery, clear link-level disclosures. Images have alt text and fallbacks; motion respects reduced-motion preference. No collection of visitor submissions. Privacy text describes the actual site and external destinations without inventing a legal identity.

Verify affiliate link safety and activation gating with Node tests. Run TypeScript, ESLint, production build, route/404 requests, and browser QA at desktop, tablet, and phone widths. Verify search/empty/reset, navigation, image loading, share URLs, keyboard focus, contact state, and no horizontal overflow. Create a private Sites deployment; public Amazon application access is a later owner decision.
